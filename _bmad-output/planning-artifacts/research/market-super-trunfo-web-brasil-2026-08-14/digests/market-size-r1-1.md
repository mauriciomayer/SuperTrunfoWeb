## Dimension: Market size & growth (Brasil)

### Findings

- **Claim:** Brazil's games market generates roughly USD 2.5 billion (~R$13 billion) annually as of 2025, with Brazil ranking as the largest market in Latin America and among the top 10-12 globally.
  - Source: Allcorrect Games, "Brazil Gaming Market 2025: Data, Trends and Growth" (citing GameIndustry.biz, Newzoo, BrazilGames.org, Statista), published Sept 9, 2025, accessed 2026-08-14
  - Confidence: medium (aggregator synthesizing multiple named sources, not a single primary report)
  - Class: quant-market-size

- **Claim:** A separate, independently repeated figure of R$13 billion/year for the Brazilian games market traces to a single original study ("Gameverso: Onde Tudo Está Conectado" by MindMiners) and is echoed verbatim across several Brazilian business-press outlets.
  - Source: Mercado&Consumo, "Mercado de games movimenta R$13 bi no Brasil...", published Feb 7, 2024; also Correio da Manhã, April 2026; Escola Brasileira de Games (undated); accessed 2026-08-14
  - Confidence: low-medium — one publisher (MindMiners) echoed by multiple sites, not two independent sources. Directionally consistent with the Allcorrect/Newzoo figure but not independent corroboration.
  - Class: quant-market-size

- **Claim:** Brazil has approximately 103-115 million gamers, with gaming penetration at 36.1% of population in 2025, projected to rise to 39.0% by 2030 (87.4M+ users).
  - Source: Allcorrect Games (citing Newzoo), published Sept 9, 2025, accessed 2026-08-14; player-count range corroborated independently by Correio da Manhã / SantoTech, "mais de 100 milhões de jogadores," April 2026, accessed 2026-08-14
  - Confidence: medium-high (two independently-attributed sources converge on 100M+)
  - Class: quant-market-size

- **Claim:** In the 12th edition of Pesquisa Game Brasil (PGB), 82.8% of Brazilians reported consuming digital games in 2024/2025 data collection — the highest ever recorded, up 8.9 pp YoY; 88.8% call digital games a main entertainment form; smartphones lead as platform, especially among lower-income classes; 77% of gamers spend up to R$250/month.
  - Source: Pesquisa Game Brasil (PGB), 12th edition, conducted by SX Group and Go Gamers with Blend New Research and ESPM (n=6,282 across 26 states + DF), published March 2025 (reported via GameVicio, SantoTech, Forbes Brasil), accessed 2026-08-14
  - Confidence: high — Brazil's primary industry-association-adjacent survey, methodologically documented, consistently reported across independent outlets
  - Class: quant-growth / qualitative-trend

- **Claim:** Platform revenue split in Brazil (2023 data): mobile 51.7%, console 20.5%, PC 19.4% of the games market; digital sales share of total grew from 54% (2021) to 62% (2023).
  - Source: Allcorrect Games (citing Newzoo/Statista), published Sept 9, 2025, accessed 2026-08-14
  - Confidence: medium (single aggregator article, underlying primary figures not independently re-verified this run)
  - Class: quant-market-size / quant-growth

- **Claim:** Latin America (Brazil as primary driver) saw one of the strongest YoY mobile-gaming IAP revenue growth rates globally at ~13%; Brazil has become a preferred "soft-launch" test market for global mobile game publishers, though IAP monetization share for hybrid-casual games in Brazil runs lower than major global markets.
  - Source: Sensor Tower, "State of Mobile Gaming 2025" report (via gamedevreports.substack.com and Sensor Tower blog), published 2025, accessed 2026-08-14
  - Confidence: medium-high (Sensor Tower is a primary analytics provider; obtained via secondary digest rather than the report itself)
  - Class: quant-growth / qualitative-trend

- **Claim:** Digital card games have an established, actively-played audience in Brazil distinct from Super Trunfo — e.g. UNO's mobile app has historically ranked among top downloaded card apps in Brazil, and "Truco ZingPlay" markets itself as having "1M+" Truco players in Brazil.
  - Source: AppGeek.com.br "11 melhores jogos de cartas grátis," undated; Apple App Store listing for Truco ZingPlay, accessed 2026-08-14
  - Confidence: low (marketing copy / listicle language, unaudited "1M+" claim from the app's own store listing)
  - Class: qualitative-trend / brand-signal

- **Claim:** Super Trunfo (licensed and published in Brazil by Grow since the 1970s-era Top Trumps concept) remains a widely-distributed physical product line with many active themed editions (Predadores, Animais Selvagens, Dinossauros, Países, Pixar, etc.) sold across all major Brazilian retail channels (Amazon.com.br, Magazine Luiza, Ri Happy, Mercado Livre).
  - Source: Multiple current retailer listings (Amazon.com.br "Loja Grow Oficial," Magazine Luiza, Ri Happy, Real Brinquedos), cross-checked against Wikipedia "Super Trunfo" entry for brand history, accessed 2026-08-14
  - Confidence: medium — confirms continued commercial presence/breadth as a brand-recognition proxy, but no survey-based brand-awareness or unit-sales figure was found
  - Class: brand-signal

**Overall assessment:** Brazil's general digital-games market is large, growing, mobile-dominated (~USD 2.5B/year, 100M+ players). "Digital trump-card game" is not measured as its own category by any cited firm — the reachable market must be inferred as a slice of the broader "card games"/"casual games" niche, not read off any report directly. The strongest asset found is qualitative: Super Trunfo's decades-long, multi-edition retail presence functions as a brand-recognition proxy, but no quantified nostalgia/awareness metric exists in public sources found this run.
