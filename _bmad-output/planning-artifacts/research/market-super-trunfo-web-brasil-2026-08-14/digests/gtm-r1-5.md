## Dimension: GTM & pricing dynamics (Brasil)

### Findings

- **Claim:** The dominant monetization model for Brazilian casual mobile games in 2025 is free-to-play with in-app purchases, increasingly structured around battle passes, seasonal events, and cosmetic items rather than simple pay-walls.
  - Source: Negócios e Games, "O Mercado de Jogos Mobile no Brasil em 2025: Faturamento, Tendências e Participação de Mercado," published 2025, accessed 2026-08-14
  - Confidence: medium
  - Class: pricing-model

- **Claim:** Brazil's mobile games segment is projected at roughly US$3.5 billion in 2025 within an overall ~US$8 billion/year Brazilian gaming market, with mobile the preferred platform for ~44–49% of players (vs. ~24% console, ~21% PC).
  - Source: Negócios e Games, same article, published 2025, accessed 2026-08-14
  - Confidence: medium
  - Class: pricing-model

- **Claim:** Historically, Brazil has had one of the world's cheapest mobile-game user-acquisition costs — average CPI ~US$1.42, second lowest among 9 major markets behind China (~US$1.32) — but with the lowest IAP conversion rate (4.2%) among those markets, meaning cheap installs don't translate to easy monetization.
  - Source: Mobile Time (citing Liftoff's worldwide mobile gaming report, Jun 2018–May 2019), published 2019-08-29, accessed 2026-08-14
  - Confidence: medium (directionally useful; underlying data ~7 years old, well outside the 12-month freshness target — treat as historical anchor only)
  - Class: cac-benchmark

- **Claim:** itch.io is a viable free/organic distribution channel for Brazilian indie developers: no review queue, game jams (Ludum Dare, GMTK, Brackeys) cited as major organic-traffic source, active Brazilian community ("Brasileiros na Itch.io") — lower-friction alternative to Steam's $100 fee + 30% cut.
  - Source: CursoGame.dev, "Como Publicar um Jogo no itch.io: do Build à Página Pronta pra Vender," accessed 2026-08-14 (publish date unconfirmed)
  - Confidence: low-medium (blog source, no hard traffic numbers)
  - Class: channel

- **Claim:** Strong, very recent Brazilian precedent for a free browser-based multiplayer/social card-drafting-style game going viral organically via social media with zero paid acquisition: "7a0" (World Cup team-drafting web game, 7a0.com.br), built by a solo/small creator, launched 2026-06-03, hit 4M+ views on its announcement post within 72h, sustained 100k+ unique users/hour at peak (45k/hour overnight), 10M+ total visits from BR + Argentine users — monetized only via voluntary Ko-fi donations, no ads/IAP, distributed purely via X/Twitter virality, later amplified by streaming coverage (CazéTV's CopaZona).
  - Source: GKPB – Geek Publicitário, "7a0: brasileiro cria jogo simulador de Copa do Mundo e game viraliza," published 2026-06-10, accessed 2026-08-14
  - Confidence: high
  - Class: precedent-case
