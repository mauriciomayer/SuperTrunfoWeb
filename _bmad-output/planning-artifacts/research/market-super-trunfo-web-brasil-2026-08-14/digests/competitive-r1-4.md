## Dimension: Competitive landscape (Brasil)

### Competitors/substitutes identified

**Super Trunfo Battle Cards (official Grow-licensed app)** — mobile app bringing physical Grow Super Trunfo to digital, multiple decks, Facebook-based "online multiplayer". Free + IAP for decks/power-ups. ABANDONED: last update Sept 8 2015 (v2.6.5, dev "Dynadel Digital Ltda"); official microsite supertrunfo.growgames.com.br no longer resolves. Source: APKPure, accessed 2026-08-14. Confidence: medium.

**SUPER TRUNFO ANIMAIS** — Android app, animal theme, dev "TI Unisagrado" (university/small studio, not Grow). Monetization/multiplayer/traction not confirmed (Play listing truncated). Source: Google Play, accessed 2026-08-14. Confidence: low.

**Super Trunfo Elementar** — Educational Android app, periodic-table theme, dev LDTT/FURB (university, not Grow). Multiplayer is local-network only (same Wi-Fi, room host/join), not real internet multiplayer, no AI opponent. Source: Google Play, accessed 2026-08-14. Confidence: low.

**All Rider Trunfo (Playtronic)** — Trunfo mechanic reskinned around Kamen Rider IP, not cars. Free base + paid card packs. Shows the mechanic is reused beyond Grow's own themes by an active BR studio. Source: Google Play developer page, accessed 2026-08-14. Confidence: low.

**Top Drives — Car Race Battles (Hutch Games)** — closest existing product to "digital car-themed trunfo-style battle game" internationally: compare real car-spec cards, deck-building, real-time PvP/multiplayer events, partnership with automotive site EVO (formal Top Trumps license unconfirmed). Free + IAP (£0.99–£99.99) + lootbox-style drops. 4.6★, 24,000 ratings, actively updated (last: Aug 4 2025). Source: Apple App Store (UK), accessed 2026-08-14. Confidence: high on traction/monetization; medium/unverified on formal IP license.

**Top Trumps (Media Factory Srbija)** — minor unofficial-looking regional Android app, Serbia-themed. 100+ downloads only, abandoned since Jan 7 2017. Source: APKCombo, accessed 2026-08-14. Confidence: medium.

**Top Trump It (Winning Moves)** — official UK rights-holder companion app, card-creator/sharing tool, not a battle game. Free. ~2014-era. Source: AppAdvice, accessed 2026-08-14. Confidence: low.

**Truco Jogatina (Jogatina.com / Gazeus Games)** — BR Truco app, part of a wider platform (Buraco, Tranca, Sueca, Poker, Dominó, Bingo, Paciência). Freemium (limited free matches, VIP subscription for unlimited). Real-time multiplayer + offline AI practice. ~12,969 concurrent "players in rooms" at access time; Gazeus founded 2006, RA1000-certified (Reclame Aqui) — established BR presence. Actively operating. Source: Jogatina.com, accessed 2026-08-14. Confidence: medium.

**Truco ZingPlay (ZingPlay International)** — BR card bundle (Truco Mineiro/Paulista, Buraco, Tranca, Cacheta, Sueca, casino minigames). Free + IAP R$6.90–R$129.90. Real-time multiplayer (1v1, 2v2), no AI opponents mentioned. 4.0★, 1,700+ ratings, actively updated (last: March 26 2024). Marketing claim of "1M+ Truco lovers" is NOT corroborated by the 1,700-rating count — treat as unverified/inflated. Source: Apple App Store (BR), accessed 2026-08-14. Confidence: high on store-visible stats; low on the "1M+" claim.

### Findings (cross-cutting)

- **No dedicated, actively-maintained digital "Super Trunfo" web-based real-time-multiplayer product (car-themed or otherwise) exists.** Every Super Trunfo-branded digital product found is either the official Grow app (abandoned since 2015, dead companion site) or small non-commercial/educational apps with no visible traction. No car-themed digital Super Trunfo product was found under any publisher. Source: APKPure + Google Play/web search, accessed 2026-08-14. Confidence: medium-high on the absence claim.
- **Grow Jogos e Brinquedos confirmed as Brazilian publisher/distributor of Super Trunfo** (based on UK "Top Trumps" concept), multiple physical SKUs sold via BR retail. Formal trademark/INPI registration not checked this run. Source: Wikipedia + BR retailer listings, accessed 2026-08-14. Confidence: medium.
- **Top Drives (Hutch Games) is the nearest successful "digital car-themed trunfo-style battle game"** — not Brazilian, not explicitly Top Trumps-branded, but mechanically almost identical and the most commercially successful/actively maintained product in this adjacent space. Source: Apple App Store, accessed 2026-08-14. Confidence: high on traction; medium on genre-fit framing.
- **Generalist BR card platforms (Jogatina/Gazeus, ZingPlay) are the most direct attention substitutes** — real-time human multiplayer, freemium/IAP, AI opponents in Jogatina's case — but neither offers a Super Trunfo/car-battle mode, so they compete for time/attention, not features. Source: Jogatina.com + App Store, accessed 2026-08-14. Confidence: medium-high.
