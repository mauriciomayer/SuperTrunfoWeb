# Reconciliation: PRD (§1, §5, §8) vs. Research (Executive Summary + Recommendations)

## 1. Verdict

**Minor gaps.** No direct contradictions found; the PRD acknowledges the two highest-stakes findings (competitive vacuum, Grow brand/licensing risk) explicitly, and goes further than the research's own recommendation on monetization/scale restraint. Two nuances from the research are absent from the PRD without acknowledgment — neither rises to "material" given the project is hobby-stakes with no public launch or GTM in scope, but both are worth a one-line note if the PRD is ever revisited.

## 2. Gaps (findings/recommendations not reflected, or reflected without acknowledgment)

1. **Nostalgia-driven positioning (research recommendations #1 and #3, cross-insight #2) is absent from PRD §1 Vision.** The research explicitly earmarks this for "futura seção de posicionamento do PRD," and frames the strongest GTM/positioning angle as "a marca Super Trunfo que você jogava quando criança, agora online" — nostalgia + social proof, not a technical-features pitch. PRD §1 Vision instead frames the product purely functionally ("a versão digital multiplayer... que faltava existir," rules automation, real-time via browser) and §2.1 JTBD frames the family/friends angle without ever naming nostalgia or childhood memory as the hook. This is defensible — the project has no public launch or marketing motion (§5, §6.2 explicitly exclude that), so a positioning statement has no audience to reach — but the PRD is silent where the research was explicit, and doesn't say "this doesn't apply because there's no GTM in scope."

2. **PRD §1 Vision states the competitive vacuum as unambiguous validation, dropping the research's own caveat.** PRD: "o terreno em que ele pousa está genuinamente vazio... nenhuma versão digital ativa... existe hoje, nem mesmo da própria detentora da marca." Research's cross-insight #1 (one of the three findings explicitly called out as most load-bearing for the decision, echoed in the exec summary) reads the same fact differently: the vacuum "não é necessariamente uma oportunidade óbvia esperando ser capturada — pode ser também um sinal de que a demanda digital nunca foi comprovada, e não apenas 'ainda não atendida.'" The PRD reuses the vacuum fact but not the caveat attached to it. Low-stakes here since the PRD isn't using the vacuum to justify a go/no-go or investment decision — the decision to build is already made and stated as a hobby/learning project — but it's a case of citing half of a paired finding.

## 3. Worth noting (not necessarily fixing)

- **What the PRD does well:** the Grow brand/licensing risk (research recommendation #4) is the best-covered item in the whole document — referenced in the title note (§0), §4.1 notes (extended by analogy to car-manufacturer trademarks, which research didn't even raise), §5 Non-Goals, and §8 Open Question #2. The "don't over-invest in monetization/scale" recommendation (#5) is also well covered and arguably strengthened: PRD §5 forbids monetization entirely (research only said "keep it simple or absent"), and §7 adds an explicit contra-metric (SM-C1) plus RNF-2 language ("dimensionado para o uso real... não para escala pública") that operationalizes restraint in a way the research recommendation didn't spell out.
- **Unused option from research:** cross-insight #3 suggests the product could position itself between "a marca nostálgica brasileira" and "a mecânica comprovada globalmente" (Top Drives) — this framing doesn't appear anywhere in the PRD. Fine to leave out given no positioning/marketing work is in scope.
- **Open Questions §8 scope is appropriately narrower than research §9**, and that's correct, not a gap: research's open questions about TAM sizing, verbatim app reviews, and nostalgia/awareness quantification are all demand-validation questions that don't matter for a closed-circle hobby build with no monetization or public launch. The PRD correctly kept only the one research open question that still carries real (legal) risk at hobby scale — the Grow trademark status — and added its own two (which card gets the Super Trunfo flag; the FR-7 leftover-distribution rule), which are implementation questions research had no view on.
- **Staleness flag not inherited (fine to skip):** research's own staleness map marks the Top Drives and Truco ZingPlay competitor-traction claims as already expired ("vencida") at time of publication, and recommends revalidating before any "decisão de posicionamento competitivo definitiva." The PRD doesn't reference this, but since the PRD makes no competitive-positioning claims that depend on those specific stats, there's nothing to revalidate against.
